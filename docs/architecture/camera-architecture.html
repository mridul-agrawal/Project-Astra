Three things the picture says

Camera scripts only exist where there is a world. Eight of the eleven scenes carry a plain Unity camera and nothing else — there is nothing to follow, bound or snap in a menu.

One shared foundation, two follow policies. MapCamera is the same script everywhere and is now the single source of the viewport numbers, which both policies read rather than deriving again. The policies stay separate because they solve different problems: a cursor stepping whole tiles against a character moving continuously at sub-tile speed.

CameraContainment is used by the hub only, for now. CameraController keeps its own clamp because it works in Vector2Int tile space against a viewport-sized margin, not in float world space. Worth revisiting if the battle camera ever needs sub-tile positions; not worth forcing today.
