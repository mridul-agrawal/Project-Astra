6 · Seams worth knowing

  • The player is an NPC with three things bolted on. Same factory, same HubActor; then a controller added at runtime by the bootstrapper, a probe collider, and solid: false.
  • Nothing owns the player except the loader. HubLocationLoader.Player is the only reference; she is built once and moved between rooms, while the cast is destroyed and rebuilt.
  • An interactable finds the player, not the other way round. GetComponentInParent<HubPlayerController>() from whatever entered its trigger — so the interaction list is filled by the things being interacted with.
  • Two collision systems sit on one actor. A trigger circle for reach on the root, a solid box on a child on the HubSolid layer, built lazily on the first SetSolid(true).
  • Where a character stands is asked twice. The visit's authored placement, then HubVisitService.TryGetPlacement, so anything that has since moved them wins.
  • The gate is a lock, not a state. Movement and interaction both ask it every frame; a conversation or a doorway holds it, and a refused second handover is how two doors at once is prevented.
